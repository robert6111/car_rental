Car Rental System - Updates Added

1. Rental status automation
- Expired active rentals are automatically marked Completed and their cars are released as Available.
- Same-date rentals are NOT force-completed automatically. They stay Active and the system shows a manual Complete button so the user can confirm the car was really returned.

2. Loyalty discount
- Customers with 3 or more completed rentals receive a 10% loyalty discount on new rentals.
- The discount is calculated on the server and shown on the add rental page.

3. Late-payment interest
- Remaining unpaid rental balances now show late interest after the rental end date.
- Default rate: 1% per day on the unpaid base balance.
- Payment pages and payment history show rental cost, late interest, total due, paid amount, and remaining balance.

4. Damage tracking linked to renter
- Damage reports now require selecting the completed rental, not only the car.
- Damage list and damage details show the renter and rental period.

5. Registration-number search
- Added registration/customer search in rentals, active rentals, payments, damages, car list, rental reports, and payment reports.
- Car registration input now accepts international-style plate numbers.

6. Import data
- Admins can import Customers and Cars from CSV through the Import Data page.
- Customer CSV headers: full_name, phone_number, email, license_no
- Car CSV headers: reg_no, make, model, year, price_per_day, status

7. Maintenance records
- Added a Service Records list page for viewing/searching recorded service and maintenance history.

No database structure change was required for these updates.
